## Current scope and remaining evidence

- GEPA/AIME and ACE/Combee Formula retain pinned source/data and adapted model settings.
  Their current offline mechanism, ordering, reference-conformance and recovery checks
  are implementation evidence. Original-model/paper performance parity is not established.
- The four Formula continuation records remain linked to their actual historical
  runtime repairs. Current transaction/accounting tests are not a replay of old outages.
- Meta-Harness retains the three MCE datasets and candidate-code protocol. The frozen
  run used one JSON proposal call for three programs, rather than the paper's coding-agent
  search. Its custom proposer/relay must be supplied and qualified for a fresh run.
- CORAL current gates include command/container workers, independent grading, gateway
  recovery and the original circle objective. One/two-worker historical results do not
  establish a benefit from cooperation; shared-knowledge availability does not prove use.
- Native acceptance now includes interrupted checkpoint publication and retained
  uncertainty in addition to completed resume. The pinned source gates additionally
  exercise real next-episode activation and compiler-rejected candidate repair with a
  local provider. These are separate from the historical Pi v0.85.1 integration result.

Historical score definitions, selection vs last candidates, missing/failed outcomes,
cost coverage and single-seed limits remain in the [family guides](../recipes/README.md)
and [results.json](../recipes/experiments/single-seed-20261020/results.json).
No across-task ranking or statistical significance is inferred here.
